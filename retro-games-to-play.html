Games I want to emulate

Posted on Apr 19, 2024 in blogpost

I like older games a lot, I grew up playing the gameboy pokémons and the gameboy advance fire emblems on my cracked PSP. I did play a couple of actual PSP games on it as well.

The hardware

Hopefully a Retroid 4 pro, which is an android device with decent specs which should allow up to PS2 emulation. I placed an order for it a month ago and there’s no sign of it yet, but I’ve got my fingers crossed.

Update 17 May 2024: Looks like I won’t be getting the device after all. I shall be playing these on my PC instead.

Games list

I’m only listing games I haven’t played, and as you could probably tell, I haven’t played many of the classics!

SNES

  • The Mega Man X series : I’ll probably only play the first one and move on to the sequels when I’m done with the other games on the list
  • Chrono Trigger : Maybe the most well-known JRPG? I find playing RPGs a bit taxing due to how long they are, but I’ll make an exception for this and possibly one more

PlayStation

  • Castlevania Symphony of the Night : The game that put ‘vania’ in metroidvania. I played the Castlevania advance collection on my switch and this is supposed to be much better, so I’m excited.
  • Metal Gear Solid : I might play MGS2 and MGS3 later as well.

Dreamcast

  • Jet Set Radio : The graphics still seem to hold up today.
  • Crazy Taxi : I played a bit of this in an arcade 10 years ago.

GBA

  • Legend of Zelda minish cap : I got stuck at some point as a child and could not advance the story, time for revenge.
  • Metroid Fusion : I guess this puts the ‘metroid’ in metroidvania?
  • Kuru Kuru Kururin : Awesome name

N64

  • Majora’s Mask : I love the Legend of Zelda, how could you tell?
  • Mario 64 : This would be my first 3D Mario, actually.

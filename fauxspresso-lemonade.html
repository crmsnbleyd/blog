Fauxspresso Lemonade

Posted on Dec 10, 2024 in blogpost

Okay, this is actually an aeropress limeade.
This is sort of a modified version of an aeropress recipe from Coffee with April

Ingredients

  • 13 grams coffee, medium roast
  • 130 ml water
  • Half a lime (15 grams)
  • Lots of ice (at least 40 grams)
  • Sugar/sweetener to taste

The lime variety I use is Key Lime, but any will do I suppose. I have made this with either a bit of maple syrup or artificial sweetener, you don't need a lot of it to make the drink taste good. If you don’t have an aeropress, 130ml of coffee in 1:10 ratio made with any method will work.

Steps

  1. Heat up 130 ml of water in a kettle to 100℃.

  2. Grind the coffee to a medium consistency, for reference it is 5 clicks on the Timemore Go.

  3. Pour in 50ml of the hot water in the aeropress, stir it a little bit and seal the aeropress so water doesn’t pass down (you do it by putting the plunger in a little bit and then pulling it almost all the way out to create pressure that prevents the water from leaving)

  4. After 30 seconds, put in the rest of the water (100ml), and wait for 90 seconds more.

  5. Pour the coffee out of the aeropress, and mix in your sweetener.

  6. Add the ice.

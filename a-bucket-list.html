A little bucket list

Posted on Jan 07, 2024

  • Write a book of fiction, either short stories or a novel. I've always wanted to do it, reading being the one hobby I've had consistently since childhood. I don't have anything against writing non-fiction; indeed, it's the primary thing I do on this blog, but I think you either have to be a specific kind of person, or reach a specific point in your career, or be an academic to write one of those.

  • Make a videogame, it doesn't have to be a solo endeavour either. This one might not be a goal in the future, and is a pretty tenuous one presently because I don't play games all that much and thus would probably not be that good at making one either. I love programming, though, so that's why it came to mind.

  • Get good at manifesting the music I feel in my head into the real world, probably using electronic tools. Both my partner, and my sister play multiple instruments and it gets me a little jealous, if I'm being honest.

  • Improve my German at least to the level where I can read books, and then, perhaps naively, I belive my skills will just snowball. Next, I want to get really good at Malayalam, the language of my parents' families. I speak and understand and am able to read it a little, but not well. They grew up in Assam, where Assamese is spoken, and I was born there as well, making it my first language. I am unable to speak it any longer as I needed to learn Hindi pretty fast at a young age in order to live elsewhere in India, and my parents just stopped speaking Assamese around me. Ideally I'd like to be able to speak it again, but that's unlikely to happen.

  • Get good at chess. This one ties into general life skills. I have little to no patience, and it is the death of me in a game. I cannot make my mind stick around to a line of evaluation long enough to calculate what my opponent might do. My calculation is also not fast enough to play quicker formats. Will being good at chess make me smarter? Probably not!

  • Generally get healthy enough to run a marathon. I have asthma and have never been athletic as a child either. I don't like losing my breath anytime I do something slightly strenuous, so I wish to change that.

  • Learn how to do neat skateboard tricks.

  • I really want to impact a lot of lives in a positive, material way, whether it be by donating or volunteering and whatnot. This might be the most important one!

Bootstrapping Kannada and Malayalam via Devanagari (or scratch)

Posted on Aug 04, 2026

Devanagari (the script used to write Hindi and Sanskrit), the Kannada Script, and the Malayalam script are closely related, all of them being part of the Brahmic script family, also called Indic scripts.

Other languages also have scripts that are part of the same family – and this is not an exhaustive list – Assamese/Bengali, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Sinhala, and even Thai.

They share almost 90%+ of their letter sounds. The fun thing is that in the alphabet, the scripts discussed here have the letters in the same order, so drawing parallels is really easy. The order is actually based on the phonetics of the letters, with each row of consonants being a different part of the mouth the sound is made in, going back from the lips to the back of the throat

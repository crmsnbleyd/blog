Rules for making a playlist

Posted on Aug 02, 2026

Curating a collection of music for a dear friend or a crush to listen to is a time-honoured tradition, becoming accessible and a huge part of pop culture around the time cassette tapes and home-recording became possible. With the rise of digital media, we mostly make playlists on streaming services like Deezer or whatever now, sending a link to the playlist instead.

I got new rules I count 'em

  • consistent mood or spectrum of moods
  • no repeated artists
  • not too long or too short

These rules are obviously not concrete. You can break them for effect. A short playlist could be used to convey something really specific. A long playlist could be used to portray a more general mood, or just act as an accompaniment (e.g. for a road-trip) Artist-specific playlists can obviously exist, as a sort of Greatest Hits compilation.

A playlist

I've lost a bunch of playlists I made on a certain streaming platform once I migrated off it, but I made one recently for my beau. I was quite sleepy at around 1 am on a school night, so it does not tick all the boxes, but I'd just like to immortalise it in a way outside of a proprietary service. This also contains a bunch of my favourite songs (though I guess all playlists I make would?)

What I would do if I was invisible for a day

Posted on Jul 04, 2026

This prompt comes from IndieWebClub Bangalore. I have had 25 minutes to pen this down.

Frankly, logistics are the main constraint. I would hate to spend my period of invisibility on an airplane flying to a different country in order to optimise my experience, but thankfully there is much to do within the bounds of Bangalore.

Something that would be very tempting to do is go somewhere with a high density of valuable goods and start stealing stuff, but I don’t reckon the prompt was about how to extract the maximum economic benefit from my condition.

If highly illegal stuff (like say, breaking into a corrupt individual’s home in order to reveal their crimes, not that I would ever consider it in any other non-hypothetical situation) is off the table, I might go to a library, as schlocky as that may sound. I haven’t been to a proper, huge library since my university days, and would love to just go around looking at all the books available and noting down what interests me. The way one finds out what to read without a library is this neat trick called “asking people”, but I have not gotten many suggestions yet. If you’re reading this, email me book suggestions.

I am frankly ashamed of how little of an id I seem to have. I would go around petting dogs (who would not be alarmed since they’d be able to smell me), sampling small bits of food and drink from bakeries, and try to execute skateboarding tricks on some smooth paved road in someone’s private estate. I don’t know any skateboarding tricks other than pumping, but have been meaning to learn, so it’d be a great time to follow through without the fear of seeming silly and having access to a nice, smooth surface (a luxury within India).

Speaking of estates, I’m sure the super rich have some really cool artwork hidden in their homes that only their rich friends get to see. It would be hard to triage which ones to hit and in which order, but perhaps there’s a list out there of Bangalore millionaires with great taste. Magazines and e-papers thrive on such trite articles.

If there were any league football games on in the area I’d take the opportunity to get close to the field and experience the game in a way that’s hard to do from the stands or the screen. Maybe run around behind the referee.

I think the theme is I would like to partake in situations I would normally not be allowed to get close to in my civilian life? Here is where a lack of desire stymies me. I can play Persona on my switch in my home without being invisible and that’s what I’ve been feeling like doing the most lately anyway.

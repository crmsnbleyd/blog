Adding Emacs packages sucks (for me)

Posted on Aug 10, 2023 in blogpost

I love that configuring emacs is simply writing emacs-lisp code.

I also love the fact that use-package, the declarative configuration manager is officially part of emacs as of 29.0.

When I started at a new job in January 2023, I declared pseudo-.emacs bankruptcy, and started with a completely new emacs configuration focused around what I would be doing at work. It was a smashing success, to the point that it is now my primary emacs configuration, used across every device I own.

I have version-controlled it as well, using a literate org-mode file to catalogue every single package I am using.

Therein lies the problem.

Inadvertently, I have gotten stuck in a sort of minimalist, aesthetic mindset, where I would rather suffer than modify my emacs configuration and upload it to GitHub. At the time of writing, markdown-mode is not actually in my configuration, I simply ran M-: (use-package markdown-mode) and carried on. Helps that I only rarely restart emacs. I suppose that is the beauty of it.

I am much more willing to add any package I desire on something like VSCodium, where no-one can see what I choose to do with my own config and the configuration is not in the form of code.

I much prefer having my current problem, though, because emacs loads up in less than two seconds.
